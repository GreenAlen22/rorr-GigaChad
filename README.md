# Gigachad

A new survivor for Risk of Rain Returns: a memetic strongman who fights at melee range and doesn't back down. While he attacks, he takes 15% less damage and can't be stunned or knocked back.

![Gigachad](https://github.com/GreenAlen22/rorr-GigaChad/blob/main/gigachad/jump.png?raw=true)

## Skills

| Slot | Skill | What it does | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Stab | Punch ahead for 200% damage | none |
| Primary | Sparta | Kick ahead for 300% damage, stun for 1.5 s and knock back. Every third kick deals 600% | none |
| Secondary | Seismic Shift | Stone spikes ahead, 2x250% damage | 6 s |
| Secondary | Trolling | Swing for 100% and release a Troll Face that deals 150-600% to each enemy it touches and grows with each kill | 5 s |
| Utility | A Moment | Dash forward; you cannot be hit while dashing. Your next attack is a critical strike | 5 s |
| Utility | Gigamusic | +60% attack speed and +40% movement speed for 8 s, +0.5 s per kill | 20 s |
| Special | Star Fury | 25x30% punches ahead and a 300% final blow; you cannot be hit while it lasts | 8 s |
| Special | MeguMin | Chant for 2.6 s, then explode for 6000% around you. You cannot act for 2.5 s and lose half your current health unless the blast kills 10 enemies | 45 s |

With the Ancient Scepter, Star Fury becomes Cosmic Radiance (35x40% and a 500% final blow over a wider area), and MeguMin becomes MeguMax (10000% over a huge area, no health cost, 1 s of rest).

Full numbers are on the [wiki](https://github.com/GreenAlen22/rorr-GigaChad/wiki).
